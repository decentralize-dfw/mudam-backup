Mudam Café

Chiche! welcomes you to Mudam Café, a restaurant bringing together homemade food and social cohesion, while supporting access to employment, education and integration for migrants and refugees in Luxembourg.

Chiche! is open during the museum’s opening hours.

From 24 August to 4 September 2026, the kitchen will be taking a short seasonal pause.
